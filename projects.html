Course Planning Workflow

Turn venue imagery into a reviewable course plan.

  1. 01Choose the venueBegin with a calibrated template or supported venue image that provides spatial context for the course.
  2. 02Define the flowCreate the intended driving line freehand or point by point as the course takes shape.
  3. 03Refine the geometryExtend the path, edit anchors, tune smoothing, and define the usable course corridor.
  4. 04Annotate and exportAdd clipping points, gates, zones, boundaries, direction markers, and labels before exporting the completed layout.
