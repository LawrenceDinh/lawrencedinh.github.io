Privacy Policy

How Hermes Pi handles information while providing personal Gmail monitoring, notifications, and automation.

Effective date: Sept 5, 2026
01

Purpose and operator

Hermes Pi (the “Service”) is operated by Lawrence Dinh as a personal automation project. The operator’s legal name is Blank Media. Its purpose is personal Gmail monitoring, configured notifications, and related automation.

02

Google OAuth authorization

The Service accesses Google data only after the Google account owner explicitly grants requested permissions through Google OAuth. The Service accesses only data allowed by those authorized permissions.

03

Information handled

The Service may process Gmail information needed to identify new messages and evaluate monitoring conditions configured by the user. It may also maintain operational state needed to avoid duplicate notifications and records needed to troubleshoot errors.

The specific Gmail information processed depends on the permissions shown during Google authorization and the monitoring rule in use.

04

Purpose of processing

Authorized data is used to operate the Gmail monitor, create configured notifications or automation outputs, prevent duplicate alerts, troubleshoot failures, and maintain the Service.

05

Google User Data

Google user data is used only to provide functionality authorized by the user. It is not sold or used for advertising or profiling.

Google user data is not transferred to third parties except when necessary to operate functionality configured by the user, when required by law, or when authorized by the user.

Humans do not read Google user data except with the user’s explicit consent for user-requested support, when needed for security purposes, or when required for legal compliance.

The use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

06

Storage and access

Information is retained as needed to operate, troubleshoot, and maintain the Service, or as required by applicable law. No fixed retention period is promised by this policy.

07

Third-party services

The Service uses Google APIs and Gmail for the authorized Gmail-monitoring functionality. If another service is used for a configured output, this policy will be updated to identify that service and explain the related data transfer before that use begins.

08

User controls

A user can revoke Hermes Pi’s Google access through Google Account permissions, stop or remove the monitor, and contact the operator to request deletion of Service-held information. Revoking access may prevent the Service from operating.

09

Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect credentials and operational data and to reduce the risk of unauthorized access, use, or disclosure. No system or method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the Service or its data practices change. The effective date above will be revised when an update is published.

Questions, support requests, and deletion requests may be sent to twoblank1@gmail.com.
